01 — the little introduction

What is music?

More than sounds and instruments, music is a way to express what words sometimes cannot.

♪ The meaning of music

Music is an art form that combines rhythm, melody, harmony and sound to create an emotional and meaningful experience. It can tell stories, preserve culture and bring people together.

Tiếng Việt: Âm nhạc là nghệ thuật sắp xếp âm thanh và khoảng lặng thành giai điệu, nhịp điệu và cảm xúc.

♡ Why does music matter?

Music can help us relax, inspire creativity, strengthen memories and connect with other people. It accompanies celebrations, quiet moments and important memories.

Tiếng Việt: Âm nhạc giúp thư giãn, khơi gợi sáng tạo, lưu giữ kỷ niệm và kết nối con người.

🫧Emotional well-beingIt can comfort us and help us express feelings.
🧠Memory & focusRhythm and melody can support learning and recall.
🌍ConnectionSongs bring cultures and communities together.

06 — learn through listening

English listening nook.

Listen, notice the sounds, then practice describing what you hear.

🎼 A tiny listening exercise

Listen to the short instrumental sample and choose the instrument family you hear most clearly.

Tip: Listen for rhythm, melody, tempo and mood. (Nhịp điệu, giai điệu, tốc độ và cảm xúc.)

💬 Useful music vocabulary

Melody — the tune you remember (giai điệu).
Rhythm — the pattern of beats (nhịp điệu).
Lyrics — the words of a song (lời bài hát).
Instrument — something used to make music (nhạc cụ).
Genre — a style or category of music (thể loại).

Try saying: “This song has a gentle melody and a slow rhythm.”
