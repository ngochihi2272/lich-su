01 / THE BASICS

What is music?

More than sounds and instruments, music is a way to express what words sometimes cannot.

♪ The meaning of music

Music is an art form that combines rhythm, melody, harmony and sound to create an emotional experience. It tells stories, preserves culture and brings people together.

♡ Why music matters

Music can help us relax, inspire creativity, strengthen memories and connect with other people. It accompanies celebrations, quiet moments and unforgettable memories.

☁️Emotional well-beingMusic can comfort us and help us express feelings.
✦Memory & focusRhythm and melody can support learning and recall.
♡ConnectionSongs bring cultures and communities together.
